# Olist E-Commerce: Business Insights & Recommendations

This report presents the key business insights and recommendations derived from an analysis of the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset. The analysis focuses on three main areas: **Sales & Revenue Performance**, **Delivery & Operational Efficiency**, and **Customer Satisfaction**. 

## **Sales & Revenue Performance**
### Key Findings
- Olist generated `R$13.22 million` in product sales across `96,478` delivered orders, with an average order value of `R$137.04`. Freight charges contributed approximately `R$2.20 million`, bringing the total transaction value to approximately `R$15.42 million`.
- Monthly product sales generally followed an upward trend, reaching a peak of approximately `R$988K` in November 2017. This coincided with the highest monthly volume of delivered orders, at `7,289` orders. This suggests that higher order volume was an important contributor to the month's overall sales performance.
- `Health & Beauty` leads the top 10 product categories in sales, generating approximately `R$1.23` million, followed by `Watches & Gifts`, which ranks second despite having a lower order volume. `Bed, Bath & Table` records the highest order volume among the top-performing categories, with `9,272` orders.
- Sao Paulo dominates sales performance, generating approximately `R$5.07 million` in product sales from `40,501` delivered orders, significantly outperforming all other states. Rio de Janeiro ranks second, with approximately `R$1.76 million` in sales, followed by Minas Gerais, which generated approximately `R$1.55 million`. This ranking remained consistent throughout the 2016–2018 analysis period, with Sao Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and Minas Gerais consistently occupying the top three positions in both product sales and delivered order volume.
- Credit cards were by far the most commonly used payment method, accounting for `74,304` delivered orders and approximately `R$12.10 million` in total payment value. In contrast, debit cards were the least frequently used payment method, representing only `1,485` delivered orders and approximately `R$208.42K` in total payment value.
  
### Recommendations
1. Prioritize evaluating high-revenue categories such as Health & Beauty and Watches & Gifts for opportunities to expand product selection, maintain inventory availability, and improve sales performance.
2. Continue prioritizing key markets such as Sao Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and Minas Gerais, which consistently generate the highest sales and order volumes. Maintain strong product availability and delivery capabilities in these states while investigating opportunities to expand further into other regions.
3. Given the dominance of credit card transactions, maintain a convenient and reliable payment experience for credit card customers. Consider targeted promotions or payment incentives to encourage purchasing activity. 

## **Delivery & Operational Efficiency**
### Key Findings
- Of the `96,470` delivered orders with valid delivery dates, `7,826 (8.11%)` arrived after their estimated delivery dates. Among the years analyzed, 2018 recorded the highest late delivery rate at `9.37%`, indicating a higher proportion of delayed deliveries compared with the other years.
- The average time from purchase to customer delivery was `12.50` days across the analyzed orders. Notably, average delivery time decreased consistently over the analysis period, from `19.71` days in 2016 to `12.98` days in 2017, reaching `12.06` days in 2018. This indicates an improvement in delivery speed over time.
- Delivery performance varied considerably across Brazilian states. Roraima (RR) recorded the longest average delivery time at `29.34` days, followed by Amapa (AP) at `27.18` days, while Sao Paulo (SP) had the shortest average delivery time at `8.70` days.
- In terms of delivery reliability, Alagoas (AL) had the highest late delivery rate at `23.93%`, followed by Maranhao (MA) at `19.67%`. In contrast, Rondonia (RO) recorded the lowest late delivery rate at only `2.88%`. Notably, longer average delivery times did not always correspond to higher late delivery rates. For example, Amazonas (AM) had one of the longest average delivery times at `26.36` days, yet only `4.14%` of its eligible delivered orders arrived after their estimated delivery dates.
- The average time from purchase to carrier handoff was `3.22` days across the analyzed orders. This average decreased to `3.02` days in 2018, the lowest among the years analyzed. This suggests that orders were being transferred to carriers more quickly over time. 

### Recommendations 
1. Investigate opportunities to reduce overall delivery times, particularly in states with longer delivery durations, such as Roraima, Amapa, and Amazonas. Evaluate transportation routes, carrier performance, and logistics processes to identify potential inefficiencies.
2. Review the methods used to calculate estimated delivery dates, particularly in states with high late delivery rates, such as Alagoas and Maranhao. Consider incorporating regional delivery patterns, transportation times, and carrier performance into delivery estimates. More accurate estimates could help set realistic customer expectations and reduce the proportion of orders arriving later than promised.
3. Continue optimizing carrier handoff times to reduce delays before orders are transferred to carriers, helping improve the overall customer delivery experience.

## **Customer Satisfaction**
### Key Findings
- Olist received `99,224` recorded reviews, with an average review score of `4.09` out of 5. This indicates generally positive customer feedback across the analyzed orders.
- Of the 99,224 recorded reviews, `57.78%` were 5-star reviews, making this the most common rating. This was followed by 4-star reviews at `19.29%` and  1-star reviews at `11.51%`. Meanwhile, 3-star reviews accounted for `8.24%` of all reviews, while 2-star reviews represented the remaining `3.18%`. 2018 recorded the highest proportion of 5-star reviews, reaching `58.21%` of reviews that year.
- A clear difference in customer satisfaction was observed between late and on-time deliveries. Reviews associated with late deliveries had an average score of `2.57` out of 5, compared with `4.29` out of 5 for on-time deliveries. This represents a `1.72-point difference` in average review scores, with late deliveries receiving considerably lower ratings This pattern was observed `across all analyzed years and in the vast majority of states`, with Amapa being the only exception. These results highlight the strong relationship between delivery reliability and customer satisfaction.
- Customer satisfaction varied across product categories. Among categories with at least 100 recorded reviews, `Office Furniture` had the lowest average review score at `3.62` out of 5, based on 1,266 reviews. It was followed by `Fashion Male` Clothing at `3.70` (111 reviews), `Audio` at `3.83` (348 reviews), and `Construction Tools & Safety` at `3.85` (166 reviews). 

### Recommendations
1. Conduct a more detailed analysis of 1-star reviews to identify recurring complaints. Pay particular attention to categories with lower average review scores, such as Office Furniture and Fashion Male Clothing.
2. Prioritize improvements in delivery reliability, as late deliveries were associated with considerably lower customer review scores. Focus on reducing late deliveries by optimizing carrier performance, improving logistics processes, and providing more accurate estimated delivery dates.
